1. Scope

This policy explains how lihao, the individual developer of BlindOracle (“we”), processes information through the app and how you can manage it. The app stores records on your device. Sign in with Apple to use the app, including online readings, account services and access to local records. The User Agreement and Privacy Policy remain available before signing in.

2. Information processed and its purposes

Your questions, follow-ups, and responses become conversation records. Names or labels, relationships, birth dates and times, calendar choices, charting sex, time zones, birthplaces or current locations, and coordinates are used to identify participants and produce readings. The app also stores results, card draws or coin casts, conversation summaries, and unfinished forms and operations so you can review, continue, or retry them.

Language and reading-style settings are stored on the device. Service credentials are stored in the system Keychain to authenticate online requests. Information used in a conversation or reading may remain in that record even if you do not separately save the person as a profile.

3. Information sent to online AI

Permission to share data with online AI is separate from Apple sign-in. Before the first transmission to third-party AI, the app separately explains the recipients, scope, and purpose, and lets you agree and continue or decline AI use. Declining keeps drafts and leaves account services and local records available. You can withdraw permission at any time through AI data use at the top right of Chat to prevent future transmissions. Withdrawal does not automatically undo data already sent or tasks in progress, and does not guarantee that usage already incurred will be waived.

The app uses its built-in AI interpretation service for online requests. Submitting a question can immediately send the question, relevant conversation and summaries, the current profile, a directory of saved people’s names or labels and available profile fields, pending forms, and relevant past readings for planning. This can happen before you finally confirm an intake form.

Interpretations and follow-up replies send relevant participant snapshots, which may include names, birth details, places, and coordinates, along with results, cards or coin casts, and context. Summarizing a long conversation also sends the older messages being summarized. Analysis already started may continue when you switch to another app.

Stopping a reply stops this device from receiving and displaying the current response, but the server may finish a task already started and deduct credits for verifiable actual usage. Stopping reception does not guarantee cancellation or waive credit deductions, and content already sent cannot be recalled. Complete responses eligible for recovery are temporarily cached; retrieving the same cached response does not call the AI service again or deduct credits again. Questions and new messages that you have not submitted do not start a new interpretation request.

4. AI providers and third-party processing

AI interpretation requests are sent over HTTPS through our server at wellecho.cn to the DeepSeek Open Platform model API. Our server processes the question, context, profile information and response needed for that request and records the account, request time, status and necessary usage records. The server does not persist original question text; it retains request fingerprints. Full responses are temporarily encrypted for interrupted-request recovery, become unavailable for replay after 48 hours, and are cleared by scheduled cleanup. Database backups have restricted access and follow a 14-day rotation policy; the backup files themselves are not separately encrypted. Temporary responses enter backups in encrypted form and may remain until the relevant backup rotates out. The AI service receives request contents and may record technical request and usage information.

Retention, processing locations, deletion, and any training use of requests and responses depend on the provider’s current policies and API account terms. Under applicable terms, DeepSeek may retain requests and responses and use them to improve services or train models subject to de-identification and other conditions. See the provider terms below. We have not verified the specific retention, processing-region, training, or optimization arrangements applicable to this API account and do not promise that data will be excluded from those uses. Review the provider information below. Deleting local records does not delete information already sent to the service. For information held by a provider, use the request channels described in its policy or contact us for assistance.

The account service and website also log IP addresses, access times, request paths, status codes, client or browser information, referrers, and response sizes for operations, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. Access logs currently rotate daily, retaining 10 historical logs in addition to the active log; removal occurs through rotation. These logs are not used for advertising tracking.

5. Location and place lookup

When you tap “Use current location,” the app requests location access while in use, obtains the device’s position, and uses Apple’s system geocoding services to fill your chosen birthplace or current location field. Places and coordinates may be included in automatically saved form drafts, profiles, readings, and related AI requests, including some processing before final confirmation. You can decline location access, choose from the place list, or revoke permission in system settings.

The place list mainly uses bundled data. If coordinates are missing, the AI interpretation service may receive the public district name and code to assist lookup, and Apple’s system geocoding services receive the place query or location. Those system services and their partners process information under their own policies.

6. On-device storage and security

Profiles, conversations, readings, summaries, and unfinished work are stored in the app’s local directory using system file protection. Account session credentials are stored in a device-only system Keychain; AI provider keys stay on the server. The app marks its data directory as excluded from automatic system backup and currently provides no profile cloud synchronization through a developer-operated server. Protect your device and its unlock credentials.

The current app integrates no advertising tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDK. Apple’s system diagnostics, TestFlight feedback, and providers’ technical records follow their respective rules. Feedback and screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight are provided to the developer and Apple, so check them for personal information.

7. Retention and deletion scope

Local records are generally retained until you delete the relevant records or app data. You can edit or delete profiles and readings in Data, and delete individual conversations or clear all history in History. Clearing all history removes chats and unfinished forms or operations while keeping profiles and saved readings. Deleting a profile does not automatically remove its contents from older conversations or reading snapshots. Deleting a reading does not automatically remove the conversation text.

The server retains wallet, purchase, refund, credit-ledger, and usage metadata linked to the account UUID to provide the service, reconcile accounts, and resolve issues. It also retains a keyed digest of the Apple identity to prevent repeated welcome grants after deletion. The test environment additionally retains keyed digests binding a store-installation proof to a sign-in identity. These digests do not store the corresponding proof or Apple identity in plain text, but together with account and accounting records they remain pseudonymous, not fully anonymous. These accounting, usage, and abuse-prevention records currently have no single fixed automatic deletion period. Contact us with a request about your information so we can verify what still needs to be retained and what can be addressed.

Deleting local records does not delete shared files, records retained on the server, or information retained by an AI provider. Uninstalling the app normally removes local app data, but service credentials in the system Keychain may remain.

8. Your choices and information requests

You can view and edit profiles, delete records, clear chat history, and manage location permission in system settings. Stopping a reply or revoking permission does not automatically reverse completed processing or requests already sent.

Contact us below to request access, correction, a copy, or deletion of relevant personal information, or to raise a concern. We will request only information necessary to verify and address your request. Sign in with Apple sends identity credentials and the name or email Apple provides, which may be a private relay address, to wellecho.cn. The server stores the account UUID, Apple identity identifier, name or email, encrypted authorization credentials, and session digests to establish accounts and authenticate access. Purchases additionally retain product and transaction identifiers, amount and currency, and verification and refund status. We do not obtain your bank card or full payment-account details.

In Data → Account, choose Delete account and confirm. The server removes that account’s name, email, Apple identity link, authorization credentials, and sign-in sessions; disables the account; cancels unfinished tasks; and clears online response caches. UUID-linked accounting, usage, and abuse-prevention digests described above remain. The production environment also revokes known Apple sign-in grants. The test environment preserves the shared Apple grant to avoid affecting the production account. Existing copies in backups are handled through backup rotation. Account deletion does not automatically request a refund or restore the old balance to a newly registered account.

Local records are independent of accounts. Signing out, switching or deleting an account does not automatically delete or separate local records. Signing in again restores access to this device’s records. Remove local data on the device before deleting your account if needed.

9. Minors and other people’s information

Obtain appropriate permission before entering someone else’s information. Minors should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian and avoid submitting sensitive information to AI. If you or a guardian discover inappropriate disclosure, contact us so we can help address it within the scope of local storage and third-party processing.

10. Policy updates

We will update this policy and its revision date when features or information handling change. We will follow applicable requirements for additional notice or separate consent. The current version is always available at the bottom of Settings.
