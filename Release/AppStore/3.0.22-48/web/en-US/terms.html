2. Purpose and limitations of readings

BlindOracle is for exploring traditional practices, entertainment, and personal reflection. Calculations and AI responses may contain errors, omissions, or invented details. A direct or humorous tone does not establish factual accuracy. Readings cannot guarantee future events, another person’s thoughts, or the outcome of a relationship.

Use real-world information and your own judgment. Do not rely on readings for medical, legal, investment, personal safety, or other consequential decisions. Seek qualified professional help when appropriate.

3. Your information and responsible use

You choose which information to provide and can mark information as unknown. Some readings require particular details to work. Use the app lawfully, respect privacy and intellectual property, and do not use it for fraud, harassment, infringement, illegal activities, or interference with the service.

5. Credits and in-app purchases

The app offers consumable credit packs, currently configured at CNY 6 per pack. Available products and the final price are shown by the App Store before payment. This is not a VIP plan or subscription, and there is no automatic renewal. Credits are used for AI readings and deducted based on verifiable actual input and output usage; reasoning usage already included in output is not counted twice. Credits have no expiry date, but use remains subject to account status, service availability, and reasonable security and usage limits.

Purchases are credited after Apple and server verification, once per transaction. Credits belong to the server account signed in at purchase; switching accounts does not transfer the balance. Adding credits increases the existing balance and makes the combined remaining balance the progress bar’s new 100%; it does not restore all historical credits. The first successful Apple sign-in grant is available once per Apple identity. Deleting and registering again does not grant it again.

If payment succeeds but credits do not appear, use the account page’s purchase check or contact support before paying again. Apple receives and reviews refund requests; approval and related rights are governed by applicable rules. This agreement does not limit statutory rights. After receiving verified refund information, the server reverses the corresponding credits. If those credits have been used, a balance discrepancy may remain, new tasks may be restricted, and later paid credits may first offset that discrepancy. Deleting an account does not request a refund. Check remaining credits and unfinished transactions before deletion.
