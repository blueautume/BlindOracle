Start with a specific question

Use conversation to sort through what is on your mind, confirm the relevant details, and review the reading. The app combines traditional practices, local rule-based calculations, and AI assistance for reflection. It cannot guarantee future events or another person’s thoughts.

A process you can review

Charts, card draws, and coin casts are calculated on the device. Relevant structured results are sent for AI interpretation. View and manage profiles, conversations, and readings separately. Unknown information should remain uncertain rather than be invented for a definite answer.

Understand how information is used

Sign in with Apple first. Online AI requests pass through wellecho.cn to DeepSeek and may include questions, context, participant details, and locations. Complete responses are encrypted and cached for 48 hours to recover interruptions. Local profiles are not automatically synchronized as cloud profiles. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Replenish when needed, without renewal

The consumable credit pack is currently configured at CNY 6; available products and the final price are shown by the App Store. Credits add to your balance and have no expiry date. There is no VIP plan or automatic renewal. The combined remaining balance after a grant becomes the new 100%; see the Terms for usage and refund rules.

Made for entertainment and reflection

Both AI and rule-based calculations can be wrong. Use real-world information and your own judgment. Do not rely on readings for medical, legal, investment, personal-safety, or other consequential decisions.
