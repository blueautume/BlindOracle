How do I begin?

Sign in with Apple first. Restoring a saved session also needs online verification. Submit a specific question in Chat, review the details needed, and complete any card draw, number entry, or coin cast shown. Planning can send relevant questions and information before final form confirmation; the Privacy Policy explains the scope.

How do I allow or withdraw AI data use?

Permission to share data with online AI is separate from Apple sign-in. Before the first transmission to third-party AI, the app separately explains the recipients, scope, and purpose, and lets you agree and continue or decline AI use. Declining keeps drafts and leaves account services and local records available. You can withdraw permission at any time through AI data use at the top right of Chat to prevent future transmissions. Withdrawal does not automatically undo data already sent or tasks in progress, and does not guarantee that usage already incurred will be waived.

Under applicable terms, DeepSeek may retain requests and responses and use them for service optimization or model training subject to de-identification and other conditions. The specific arrangements for this API account remain unverified. See the Privacy Policy and provider terms.

What do credits and 100% mean?

Tap the account card in Data to see the remaining allowance and account settings. The consumable pack is currently configured at CNY 6; available products and the final price appear in the App Store before payment. It is not a VIP plan or subscription and does not renew automatically. Credits add to the existing balance, and the combined remaining balance becomes the new 100%. Credits have no expiry date. The welcome grant is available once per Apple identity, including after deletion and re-registration.

What if payment succeeds but credits do not appear?

Open Data → Account → Replenish allowance and tap Check purchases. Stay online while verification completes. Use the same server account signed in at purchase; changing accounts does not transfer credits. Do not pay again while approval, verification, or confirmation is pending. If needed, email us the redacted message and purchase time.

How do I request a refund?

Apple receives and reviews refund requests. Approval and related rights are governed by applicable rules. See Apple’s official refund guidance below. Verified refund information causes the server to reverse the corresponding credits; credits already used may leave a discrepancy and restrict new tasks. Account deletion does not request a refund.

What happens if I stop a reply or lose the connection?

Stopping stops reception and display on this device. The server may finish a task already started and deduct credits for verifiable usage, so stopping does not guarantee that credits will be waived. Complete responses eligible for recovery are encrypted and cached for 48 hours. Retrieving the same cache does not call the AI or deduct credits again. Retry within the original task after an interruption; recovery may be limited after expiry, invalid output, or other errors.

How do I remove local records?

Edit or delete profiles and readings in Data. Delete conversations or clear all history in the history sidebar. Records and snapshots are separate; clearing chats does not remove profiles or independent readings. Signing out, switching, or deleting a server account does not clear or separate local records by account. Signing in again restores access to this device’s records.

How do I delete my server account?

Open Data → Account, tap Delete account, read the confirmation, and confirm. The server removes the name, email, Apple identity link, authorization credentials, and sessions; disables the account; cancels unfinished tasks; and clears online response caches. Necessary UUID-linked accounting, usage, and abuse-prevention digests remain and are not fully anonymous. Production revokes known Apple grants; the test environment preserves the shared grant to protect the production account. Backup copies rotate out. Remove unwanted local records and check balances and pending purchases first. Deletion does not request a refund or restore the old balance to a new account. Email us if you cannot complete the process.

Must I provide my location and complete birth details?

You can decline location access and choose from the place list. Mark unknown information as unavailable in the interface; some methods require specific details. Places and coordinates entered may be sent in relevant AI requests. Revoke system location permission in Settings.

How do I change languages?

Open Data → Settings → Language and choose System Default, 简体中文, or English. Changing the interface language does not translate saved records.

How do I report a problem or inappropriate AI response?

Email the app version, system version, steps to reproduce, and a redacted message. AI responses can contain errors or invented details. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, investment, personal-safety, or other consequential decisions.
