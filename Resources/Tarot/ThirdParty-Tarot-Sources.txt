Tarot corpus and classic card images
====================================

Bundled corpus
--------------
Repository: https://github.com/LindseyB/tarot-api
Pinned revision: 319ce18f1b583035e00c63854a2a405a0088bc73 (2026-06-09)
Source: tarot.json
Repository license: MIT, preserved in ThirdParty-Tarot-MIT.txt.
The upstream README credits the initial tarot corpus to dariusk/corpora (CC0):
https://github.com/dariusk/corpora

BlindOracle adapts the data into a compact offline schema with stable suit/rank
identifiers and Chinese card names. Original English upright/reversed keywords
are retained. The source misspelling "The Charriot" is normalized to "The Chariot".
These are reference interpretations, not observed facts or event probabilities.

Classic Rider-Waite-Smith card illustrations
------------------------------------------
Original deck: Rider-Waite-Smith (1909), illustrations by Pamela Colman Smith.
Source files: public/cards/*.png in the pinned repository above.
The repository README identifies its source asset pack:
https://luciellaes.itch.io/rider-waite-smith-tarot-cards-cc0
Author/curator: Luciella Elisabeth Scarlett (luciellaes).

The curator describes these as scans of the original Rider-Waite-Smith deck,
sourced from Wikipedia, slightly cleaned, resized to 300x527 pixels and given a
small black border. The source page identifies the original illustrations as
public domain under the curator's assessment in Australia and asks users to
assess their own jurisdictions. The curator explicitly releases the separate
cardback design under CC0. BlindOracle bundles only the 78 classic card fronts;
its on-screen card back is drawn in SwiftUI, not copied from a modern deck.

Bundled images retain the source dimensions and were converted to JPEG at
quality 82 to reduce app size. They are classic scans, not AI-generated or a
modern commercial deck. Per-file original/bundled SHA256 hashes and exact
source paths are recorded in source-manifest.json.

Reproduction
------------
Run python3 Scripts/fetch_tarot_resources.py from the BlindOracle directory.
This fetches the fixed upstream revision and recreates the JSON and JPEG files.
The installed app loads all cards and images from its own bundle and makes no
network request for tarot data or card artwork.
