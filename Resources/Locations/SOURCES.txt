GeoNames supplies WGS84 representative points for administrative regions,
not exact birth or street addresses. This catalog extracts a subset, matches
Mainland names and parent codes, and reorganizes the display hierarchy.
The GeoNames record ID is preserved alongside each point. No coordinates are
inferred from nearby places when the administrative record cannot be matched.

Hong Kong covers all 18 districts. Macao covers seven parishes and Cotai,
shown as a selectable area. Taiwan covers 22 counties/cities and 368
townships, county-administered cities and districts.
Hong Kong labels were checked against the Digital Policy Office:
https://www.digitalpolicy.gov.hk/en/our_work/data_governance/policies_standards/interoperability_framework/code_lists/district_council_code_chi/index.html
Macao parish labels were checked against the cadastral map:
https://cadastre.gis.gov.mo/MGSP_Cad/chn/bak/main20150917.html
